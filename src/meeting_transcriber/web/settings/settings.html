Meetings
Every meeting gets its own folder here, with the recording, transcript, summary and screenshots.
Happens after the transcript is saved. The original is only deleted once the compressed copy checks out.
Microphone and system audio
Muted audio is not recorded. You can mute or unmute at any time, even between meetings, from the floating bar or with the shortcuts.
Screenshots
The area you select is saved at this share of your screen’s real resolution (sizes shown for your main display). Smaller screenshots keep the summary request light.
Floating bar
Off by default: the bar stays out of your screenshots and out of what others see when you share your screen. Takes effect right away.

Speakers

Style
A style is the set of instructions Claude follows when writing a summary. You can also change it from the floating bar.
Claude
Summaries use your Claude subscription through the Claude Code CLI. The transcript and your screenshots are sent to Claude.

Global shortcuts
They work in any app, and the mute shortcuts switch back and forth. Click a box, then press the combination (it needs Ctrl, Option, Shift or Command). Esc cancels.
In the notes panel
Puts the recording time ([12:34]) at the cursor, same as the Add time button. Only works while the notes panel is open.
